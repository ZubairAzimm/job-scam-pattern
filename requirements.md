# Requirements

## Functional requirements (what the system must DO)
1. The system shall flag the fraud job posting.
2. The system can differentiate between real and fake job posting.
3. The system should show the category of the job posting either fake or real.

## Non-functional requirements (how the system must PERFORM)
1. The system shall categorise the real and fake job posting.
2. The system shall show the statistic of user applied for the job.
3. The system shall show the suspicious activity of the fake postings.
